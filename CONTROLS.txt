NOTE: inherited from the Infinity Blade 1 wrapper. Screen positions used by

Infinity Blade controller mapping

NORMAL MODE (cursor hidden)
Left stick flick   One short combat swipe from screen center
Right stick flick  One short camera swipe from screen center
A                 Tap screen center
B                 Hold shield
Y                 Sword button (top left)
X                 Magic button (top right)
ZL                Dodge left
ZR                Dodge right
+                 Pause
-                 Unpause

CURSOR MODE (press R to toggle)
Left stick         Move cursor
Right stick flick  Short camera swipe
A                 Select at cursor
B                 Hold the bottom-right touch target
ZL                Select at cursor
ZR                Select at cursor
L                 Recenter cursor

BOTH MODES
D-pad direction   One smaller swipe in the pressed direction
R                 Toggle cursor mode

The physical touchscreen remains active.
